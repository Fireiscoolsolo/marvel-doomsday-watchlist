How to watch

This order groups films and seasons by story, so release dates sometimes jump backward. Notes mention basic premises, character names, and where to save the credits scenes.

  1. The original Avengers20 entries · ≈44h 00m

    Follow the founding heroes into the Infinity Saga. Save the first credits scene from Captain Marvel until after Infinity War, and the Black Widow credits scene until after Endgame.

  2. After Endgame and the multiverse8 entries · ≈32h 59m

    Start with the TVA, then follow the consequences of Endgame for Wanda, Sam, and Peter. Keep No Way Home ahead of Multiverse of Madness.

  3. Wakanda, new heroes, and new teams10 entries · ≈24h 21m

    These stories follow several parallel branches. Watch Wakanda Forever before the Wakanda anthology in this guide, and follow Sam and Yelena’s earlier stories into the new team films.

  4. Fox X-Men and Deadpool14 entries · ≈28h 03m

    Meet the original team first, then follow the younger cast into the timeline crossover. Finish with Deadpool & Wolverine after the TVA arc. Release dates help distinguish the films when the story changes timelines.

  5. Daredevil and Peter Parker6 entries · ≈50h 18m

    Finish the original Netflix seasons before Born Again. Matt’s situation at the start of Season 3 follows an off-list crossover, The Defenders. Season 3 provides enough context to resume here.

  6. Other universes and animated branches9 entries · ≈35h 10m

    These are parallel viewing tracks. Venom and Spider-Noir follow Sony stories. What If…? explores alternate MCU outcomes. X-Men ’97 continues the 1990s animated series and has its own timeline.

  7. The final Doomsday lead-in2 entries · ≈9h 04m

    Finish with Marvel’s First Family and the last chapter of Vision’s story. If you revisit any credits scenes before Doomsday, start with Thunderbolts* and First Steps.

  8. Add more movies and shows

    Open Other titles in the catalog at the bottom to add movies and shows to your watchlist. Sign in or create an account to save your additions.
